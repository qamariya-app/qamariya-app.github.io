Support

Qamariya helps you plan your day around prayer: prayer times, gentle reminders, the Qibla, the Quran and simple guides. Need help or found a mistake? Email pericprew@gmail.com — we usually reply within 2 working days. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and city.

Frequently asked questions

My prayer times differ from my local mosque.

Go to Prayer › Prayer settings and choose the calculation method used in your country (for example Umm al-Qura, Diyanet, JAKIM, Kemenag, Karachi or ISNA) and the Asr method (standard or Hanafi). You can also adjust each prayer by a few minutes to match your mosque's timetable.

I don't hear the adhan reminder.

Check that notifications are allowed for Qamariya (iPhone Settings › Notifications), that the ring/silent switch is not on silent, and that a Focus mode is not hiding them. iOS limits notification sounds to 30 seconds, so the reminder plays the opening of the adhan; tap it to hear the full adhan in the app. Open the app once every few days so it can schedule the next reminders.

The Qibla compass looks wrong.

Move away from metal objects and magnets, then move your phone in a figure-eight to calibrate the compass. Allow precise location for the most accurate direction.

Is there a translation of the Quran in my language?

Translations come from QuranEnc.com and are shown unmodified with the translator's name. If your language has no licensed translation yet, the app tells you and offers the closest available one.

How do I start or cancel Qamariya Pro?

Open Me › Qamariya Pro. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial on the monthly and yearly plans. To cancel, open iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Qamariya; cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid being charged. Prayer times, reminders, the Qibla, the Quran and the guides stay free.

I reinstalled the app or changed phone.

Tap Restore purchases on the Qamariya Pro screen (or Me › Restore purchases) while signed in with the same Apple ID. Your logs and bookmarks are stored only on the old device; there is no cloud copy in this version.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your device: erase it in Me › Data & privacy, or delete the app. We hold no copy. See the privacy policy.

有問題或發現內容錯誤，請寫信到 pericprew@gmail.com，請附上 iPhone 型號、iOS 版本與所在城市。

  • 禮拜時間和清真寺不同：到「禮拜 › 設定（右上角）」選擇你所在國家使用的計算法與晡禮算法，也可逐項微調分鐘數。
  • 聽不到宣禮提醒：確認已允許通知、沒有開靜音或專注模式。iOS 通知聲音最長 30 秒，點開提醒即可在 App 內聽完整宣禮；請每隔幾天打開 App 一次以排好接下來的提醒。
  • 朝向不準：遠離金屬與磁鐵，以「8」字形移動手機校準，並允許精確位置。
  • Pro 與取消訂閱：「我的 › Qamariya Pro」；月訂與年訂新用戶有 7 天免費試用。取消請到 iPhone 設定 › 你的名字 › 訂閱項目，於試用或當期結束前 24 小時取消即不扣款。
  • 換手機：用同一個 Apple ID，在 Pro 畫面或「我的 › 恢復購買」即可取回 Pro；紀錄只存在原本的手機上，這個版本沒有雲端備份。
