Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · 繁體中文

This policy explains what the iOS app Qamariya (“the app”) does with information. The app is made by an independent developer, YUN TING LEE (“we”). Questions: pericprew@gmail.com.

In short
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We never sell or share your data for advertising.
  • Your location is used on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla. It is never sent to us (iOS may ask Apple’s geocoding service for your city name).
  • Your prayer log, tasbih counts, bookmarks, journal and settings are stored on your device.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We do not collect personal data: nothing you enter in the app is sent to us.

1. Information stored on your device

Settings, prayer log, tasbih sessions, Quran bookmarks and reading progress, journal entries, sadaqah notes and downloaded recitations are kept in the app's storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also erase them in Me › Data & privacy.

2. Location

If you allow location access, the app reads your approximate position on the device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, and to notice when you have travelled to another city. Coordinates are not sent to us. When you tap “Use my location”, iOS looks up the name of the nearest city using Apple’s geocoding service, which Apple processes under its own privacy policy. You can instead pick a city from the built-in offline list, which works fully offline.

3. Notifications

Prayer reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push notification servers.

4. Content downloaded from the internet

To show Quran translations and play recitations, the app downloads files from QuranEnc.com and the Islamic Network CDN (cdn.islamic.network). These requests contain no personal information from the app, but like any internet request they reveal your IP address to those services, which process it under their own policies.

5. No account, no cloud copy

The current version has no accounts and does not upload your records anywhere. If a future version offers optional sync between devices, it will be off by default, this policy will be updated before it is offered, and you will be able to delete any synced data in the app.

6. Purchases

Qamariya Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and the free trial; we never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase directly with Apple (StoreKit) on your device — we do not use any third-party purchase service and we do not receive your purchase history. Apple's handling of purchases is described in Apple's privacy policy.

7. What we do not do

  • No advertising or advertising identifiers (IDFA), no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal information.

9. Children

The app is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone.

10. Your rights

You can access, export or delete your on-device data in the app. For any other request, write to pericprew@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
